The variables

What actually determines your home's value.

Nine things move the number. A model can approximate three of them.

01

Location and neighborhood

Not the ZIP code — the street, the section, and the attendance zone, which can split a single subdivision.

02

Lot size and positioning

Corner, cul-de-sac, greenbelt, or backing to a road. Two identical floor plans do not carry identical lots.

03

Square footage and floor plan

Total area matters less than how it is arranged and whether the layout suits how buyers here live now.

04

Condition

Roof age, systems, and finish level. The variable no public record can report and every buyer prices in.

05

Renovations and upgrades

What was done, when, and how well. Some return most of their cost at resale; several return very little.

06

Architecture and features

Character, ceiling height, natural light, outdoor living, and the details that make a house memorable at showing three of five.

07

Recent comparable sales

Closed sales that a buyer, a lender, and an appraiser would all accept as genuinely comparable to yours.

08

Active competition

What a buyer considering your home would also tour this weekend, and how yours reads beside it.

09

Current buyer demand

Showing activity and absorption in your price band right now — not what the market did last spring.

The deliverable

Know where your home stands before you make your move.

The TwelvePoint Home Value Report is a written analysis prepared for your specific property — not an instant, computer-generated figure. It is built to be read, questioned, and kept.

Plenty of the people we prepare these for are not selling this year. Knowing where you stand is useful on its own.

  • Analysis of active competitionThe homes a buyer would weigh against yours right now, and where yours sits among them.
  • Recommended pricing rangeA range with the logic attached, so you can judge the reasoning and not just the number.

A comparative market analysis is an opinion of value prepared by a real estate license holder. It is not an appraisal.

Common questions

Before you ask

Is the valuation free?

Yes — there is no cost and no obligation. It is how we introduce ourselves, and many of the people we prepare one for are not selling this year.

How is this different from an online estimate?

An automated estimate is built from public records and past sales. It cannot see condition, updates, lot position, or what is competing with your home right now. A licensed broker associate who works this market prepares yours by hand, and shows the reasoning.

Do I need to be ready to sell immediately?

No. Knowing where your home stands is useful whether you are listing next month or simply weighing options. Tell us your timeline on the form and we will treat it accordingly.

What information do you need about my property?

The address is enough to begin. Square footage, bedroom and bathroom count, condition, and any renovations make the range tighter — but leave blank anything you are unsure of.

Can renovations or upgrades affect my home's value?

Considerably, and not always in the direction people expect. Some improvements return most of their cost at resale and others return very little. Where it is relevant, the report flags which work is worth doing before listing.

What if my property is outside Houston?

We work Houston and the surrounding areas. Send the address and we will tell you honestly whether it is somewhere we know well enough to give you a useful answer.
