The property

An acre and a quarter, and nobody behind you.

Built in 2019 on a wooded cul-de-sac lot backing to a green belt, this one keeps its distance from the neighbors — which out here is most of the point.

Inside, the plan opens the way people actually live: a kitchen that runs into the family room rather than hiding behind a wall, a first-floor primary with its own sitting area, and a second-floor game room with a wet bar and a covered balcony over the back yard.

The back of the house is where it earns the price. A covered outdoor kitchen, a heated pool and spa, and enough remaining lawn for a real yard, not a strip. Mature pines along the rear line mean the view stays yours.
