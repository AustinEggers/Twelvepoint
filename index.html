The signature

The Twelve
Point Standard

Twelve commitments. Put in writing.
Every listing. Every client. Every time.

Not a philosophy or a marketing promise — a standard you can hold us to.

01

Written Pricing Analysis

Comparable sales, days on market, and a recommended range — on paper, before we ever discuss signing.

02

Professional Photography

Every listing, every price point. No phone photos, no exceptions, no upcharge.

03

Dedicated Property Marketing

Your property gets a dedicated web page we can put behind paid traffic — not just an MLS entry.

04

Same-Day Communication

Seven days a week. If we can't answer fully, you still hear from us the same day.

05

Meaningful Showing Feedback

We chase down every agent who walks through and pass along what they actually said.

06

Weekly Performance Reporting

Traffic, saves, showings, and what we're changing this week. You never have to ask.

07

Staging Consultation

A walkthrough before photos, room by room, included in our side of the transaction.

08

Aerial & Video Marketing

Drone and walkthrough video on acreage and on anything where the land is part of the story.

09

Vetted Vendor Network

Roofers, foundation, HVAC, and cleaners we've used ourselves — and we coordinate the scheduling.

10

Direct Agent Representation

The person who listed your home is the person on the phone when the offer comes in. Nothing is handed off.

11

Contract-to-Close Management

We read the report with you, price the repairs, and hold the other side to the timeline.

12

Continued Client Support

Contractor questions, tax protests, refinance timing. The relationship doesn't end at the funding table.
