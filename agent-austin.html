About

As a Texas Broker Associate and founder of TwelvePoint Realty Group, Austin believes real estate is about more than buying or selling a property — it's about finding a place that truly feels like home. His approach combines elevated service with the genuine hospitality, trust, and personal relationships that define Texas living. From luxury estates and homes on acreage to established neighborhoods and growing communities, Austin provides every client with knowledgeable guidance and service tailored to their individual goals.

Known for being straightforward, responsive, and committed to his clients, Austin takes pride in making every transaction feel personal. Whether representing a first-time homebuyer, marketing a luxury estate, or helping a family find more room to put down roots, his goal remains the same: protect his clients' interests, provide exceptional service, and build relationships that last long after closing.

At TwelvePoint Realty Group, Austin is building a real estate group rooted in Texas values and focused on a higher standard of service — where local expertise, integrity, and genuine relationships still matter.
