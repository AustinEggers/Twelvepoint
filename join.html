Why this team

You get a standard to sell with, not a script.

The hardest part of a listing appointment is proving you will actually do what you say. The Twelve Point Standard is written down and published, so you can hand a seller the page and let them hold you to it.

That is the pitch, and it is the same one every agent here uses. It works because it is specific and because we do not make exceptions on price point — a $250,000 house gets the same twelve as an estate.

What you get

You will not be figuring it out alone.

A small team means the support is real rather than a page on an intranet. Here is what is actually behind you.

  • Coaching from a licensed broker. Austin is a Texas Broker Associate and takes his own calls. Pricing, contracts, negotiation, and the awkward conversations — you get a straight answer the same day.
  • Marketing materials, done for you. Professional photography on every listing, a dedicated landing page per property, aerial and video where the land is part of the story. You are not paying for it out of pocket or making it yourself.
  • A listing presentation that already works. The Twelve Point Standard is published and specific, so you walk into an appointment with something a seller can hold you to instead of adjectives.
  • Real attention on your first transactions. Someone walks the early files with you rather than pointing you at a training portal.
  • You keep your own book. Nobody here will tell you which neighborhoods you may work or whose sphere is whose.
  • Sponsored by Goldmount Real Estate Group. TwelvePoint is a team inside that brokerage, not a brokerage itself — your license hangs with Goldmount and you work on this team.

Who we can help most

Wherever you are, there is a next level.

  • Newly licensed. The twelve points work as a curriculum as much as a promise — you learn the job by doing it properly from your first listing.
  • Producing, and ready for more. If the ceiling right now is your own time, handing photography, marketing, and listing presentation to the team is what raises it.
  • Building on acreage and custom homes. Land, ranch, and rural property is a real part of the business here, and it is not the same job as a subdivision resale. That expertise is worth having behind you.
  • Wanting to be known for something. The standard is published under your name too. Agents who lean on it tend to win the listings they used to lose.
