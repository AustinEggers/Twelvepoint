Listing checklist

The twelve points are advertised publicly, so every one of them has to actually happen on every listing. This is that promise as a sequence.

  1. Before the agreement is signed

    Written pricing analysis prepared — comparable sales, active competition, and a recommended range with the reasoning shown.

  2. Before photography

    Staging consultation walked room by room. Repairs and decluttering agreed while there is still time to do them.

  3. Photography day

    Professional stills. Aerial and video where acreage or the land itself is part of the story.

  4. Going live

    MLS entry proofed, property marketing scheduled, and the seller told the date and time it goes active.

  5. Every week it is on the market

    Written performance report: traffic, saves, showings, feedback chased from every agent, and what is changing because of it.

  6. Under contract to close

    Contract-to-close management, vendors coordinated, and the other side held to the timeline.
