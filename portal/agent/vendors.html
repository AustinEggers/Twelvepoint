Vendor List

Point nine — vetted vendor network

Only people the team has actually used. Clients are told this network is vetted, and they call these numbers at the worst moment of a transaction. A name nobody has worked with does more damage here than an empty list.

Inspectors

TREC-licensed. The one you would send your own family to.

Photographers

Stills, aerial, and video. Point two and point eight depend on this.

Stagers

Consultation and full staging, with rough pricing for each.

Foundation

Engineer reports and repair. Houston clay makes this the most-called category.

Roofing

Inspection, repair, and insurance-claim experience.

HVAC & plumbing

Fast enough to fit inside an option period.

Lenders

At least two, so a buyer can compare. Never only one.

Title & survey

Surveyors who turn work around quickly.

Cleaners & make-ready

Pre-photo and pre-closing.

A note on referrals. Under RESPA you cannot accept anything of value for sending a client to a settlement service provider — lenders, title, and insurance especially. Recommend on merit, keep more than one name per category, and be plain with clients that the choice is theirs.
